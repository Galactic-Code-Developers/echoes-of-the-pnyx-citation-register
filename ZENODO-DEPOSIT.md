## Description

Structured documentary citation register accompanying _Echoes of the Pnyx: Athenian Democracy and the Making of the American Constitutional Order_. The register records founding-era and closely related references to Athens, Greek political institutions, classical exempla, intermediary sources, rhetorical use, and verification status. It is intended to make the evidentiary path of the monograph inspectable and reproducible. The register is a documentary source inventory, not a statistical sample of everything Americans thought about ancient Greece.
